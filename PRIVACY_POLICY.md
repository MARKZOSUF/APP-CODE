## 2. Data we collect

| Data | Why | Where it is stored |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Email address, password (hashed by Firebase Auth) | Account creation and sign-in | Firebase Authentication |
| Username, name, bio, website, profile photo | Public profile | Cloud Firestore / Cloud Storage |
| Photos, videos, captions, comments, likes, saves | The content you choose to publish | Cloud Firestore / Cloud Storage |
| Direct messages | Delivering chats to the people you chose | Cloud Firestore / Cloud Storage |
| Device push token | Delivering notifications | Cloud Firestore |
| Crash and basic diagnostic data | Fixing bugs | Google Firebase |

We do **not** sell personal data, and we do not use it for third-party
advertising.

## 3. Permissions the app asks for

- **Camera / Microphone** — only when you record a photo, reel or story.
- **Photos and media** — only when you pick media to upload.
- **Notifications** — only to show activity from people you follow.
- **Biometric** — optional app lock, handled fully on-device.

## 4. Sharing

Data is processed by Google Firebase (Authentication, Firestore, Cloud
Storage, Cloud Messaging) as our infrastructure provider. We share data with
law enforcement only when legally required.

## 5. Retention and deletion

You can delete any post, story, comment or message at any time from inside the
app. Deleting your account (Settings → Account → Delete account) removes your
profile, posts, stories, comments and uploaded media. You can also request
deletion by emailing **[your-support-email]**; we respond within 30 days.

## 6. Children

Insangram is not intended for children under 13 (or the minimum age in your
country). Accounts found to belong to younger users are removed.

## 7. Your rights

Depending on where you live you may request access to, correction of, or
deletion of your data. Write to **[your-support-email]**.
